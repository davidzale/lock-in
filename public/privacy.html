LOCK IN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

LOCK IN is a personal habit, goal, finance and fitness tracker built for a single user. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

What data the app stores

  • Data you enter yourself: habits, goals, income and expenses, workouts, sleep, steps and personal growth notes.
  • Your account email, used only to sign in.
  • If you connect Google Calendar: access to your calendar events, used only to show your schedule in the app, create events you ask for, and send you reminders.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on your device and, when you sign in, in a private Supabase database protected by row-level security so that only your account can read it. Google Calendar data is read on demand and is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google user data

LOCK IN’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is never transferred to third parties and is never used to train AI models.

Deleting your data

You can export or delete your data at any time. Disconnecting Google Calendar can be done in the app settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/davidzale/lock-in.
